# Example: “我会辨方向”

```yaml
mode: PLAN
learner:
  age_or_level: 6 years old
target:
  observable_behavior: Recognize and use 上、下、左、右、东、西、南、北 in familiar scenes
  condition: Given spoken directions, pictures, and short phrases
  success_standard: At least 90% independent recognition plus one novel scene task
```

Order the content as `上/下 → 左/右 → 中 → 东/西 → 南/北`. New characters follow this order; randomization is limited to practice order and option position.

At Understand, the learner places a toy above/below and left/right, then teaches the toy what each character means. At Fluent, the learner completes two mixed sets with at most one hint. At Master, the learner repeats after a delay without labels. At Apply, the learner follows and explains a new map route.

Keep recognition evidence separate from writing evidence. A child may master recognition before being required to write every character.
