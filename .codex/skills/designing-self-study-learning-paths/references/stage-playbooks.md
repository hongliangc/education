# Stage playbooks

Apply these contracts with domain-adapter equivalents where required.

## PLAN

1. Express the final goal as observable behavior, condition, and success standard.
2. Map prerequisites and exclude unrelated scope.
3. Create stable units with objectives, content IDs, dependencies, and teaching order.
4. Define Understand, Fluent, Master, and Apply evidence for every unit.
5. Define the final novel transfer task and review policy.

Exit only when every required unit maps to the final goal.

## DIAGNOSE

1. Test important prerequisites and one representative target task without teaching.
2. Record response, confidence, reasoning, hints, and modality.
3. Mark tested nodes from evidence.
4. Start at the earliest unmet prerequisite and gate.

## UNDERSTAND

1. Retrieve prerequisite knowledge.
2. Present a concrete case and ask for a prediction.
3. Convert it to an appropriate representation.
4. Name the abstraction and contrast it with a near-miss.
5. Collect learner-produced explanation evidence or an adapter-approved equivalent.
6. Give at least three varied independent checks.

Pass: score ≥80%, independent evidence, valid explanation-equivalent evidence, and no prerequisite gap.

## FLUENT

1. Begin with closed-book retrieval.
2. Use a worked example only when needed, then fade support.
3. Run at least two independent sets.
4. Practice the weak step briefly, then return to whole tasks.

Pass: every set ≥85%, total hints ≤1, and stable unsupported performance.

## MASTER

1. Mix the unit with previously fluent units without method-revealing labels.
2. Require independent method selection, completion, and self-check.
3. Schedule a delayed assessment, normally the next day or later.

Pass: immediate score ≥90%, no hints, independent work, delayed pass, and no critical conceptual error.

## APPLY

1. Present a novel scenario.
2. Require relevant-feature identification, method selection, end-to-end work, justification, and adaptation.
3. Score result 40%, method selection 20%, explanation 20%, adaptation 20%.

Pass: total ≥80%, independent, novel-task pass, and nonzero method-selection and explanation scores.

## REVIEW

Test before showing notes. When applicable, collect explanation, core performance, and a small transfer task.

- ≥90% independent: `LENGTHEN`.
- 80–89% independent: `KEEP`.
- <80%: `SHORTEN` and reopen the appropriate gate.
- Conceptual error: `REOPEN` Understand.
- Prerequisite error: `BACKTRACK`.

Default intervals: same session, day 1, day 3, day 7, day 14, day 30.

## REMEDIATE

Quote the failing evidence, assign one primary error, choose the matching method, make the smallest useful intervention, and retest with an equivalent new task. After two failed cycles in one session, persist the gap and defer.

## EXTEND

Create the Domain Adapter from `references/contracts.md`. Confirm that every stage has evidence suited to the domain and learner. For children, include session capacity, modalities, recognition-versus-production requirements, fatigue signals, and a nonverbal explanation equivalent.
